Privacy Policy - Alpha Ross Poster

Last updated: October 2026

  1. Who we are: Alpha Ross Poster is a personal tool of Kristina (alphaross.com). It is used only by its owner.
  2. Data we handle: when the owner connects her TikTok account, the tool receives an access token, a refresh token and basic profile information (user.info.basic) to confirm which account is connected. The tool also uploads the owner's own video files and captions to her account.
  3. Storage: tokens are stored only in the owner's private GitHub repository secrets and on the owner's computer. They are not shared with anyone.
  4. We do not collect, sell or share data of other TikTok users, and we do not use any data for advertising or profiling.
  5. Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) and delete the stored secrets in GitHub.
  6. Contact: alphaross.com
