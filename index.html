Bright Hours Poster

Bright Hours Poster is a personal automation tool of Kristina (kristina-art.org). It publishes her own original music videos, created for the Bright Hours music project, to her own social media accounts, including TikTok.

The tool is not a public service and is used only by its owner. It connects to TikTok through Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the owner's explicit authorization.
